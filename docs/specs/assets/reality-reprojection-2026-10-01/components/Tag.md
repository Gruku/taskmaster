# Tag

A Technical-voice label for categories and filters; interactive tags shift `translateX(2px)` on hover — a different motion for a different meaning than buttons.

**Consumer provides:** `children`, `tone` (`default` · `signature` · `success` · `warning` · `critical`), `pill`, `interactive` or `onClick`, `onDismiss` (adds a dismiss control), `disabled`.

- Categorical, not hierarchical: no tag outranks another.
