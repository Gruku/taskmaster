# Progress

A recessed track (`surface-recessed`, `shadow-recessed`, `radius-xs`) with a grained signature fill.

**Consumer provides:** `value` (0–100) or `indeterminate`, optional `label`, `tone` (`success` · `warning` · `critical`), `size` (`sm` · `lg`).

- Indeterminate sweeps on the Pendulum. Label and value in the Technical voice.
