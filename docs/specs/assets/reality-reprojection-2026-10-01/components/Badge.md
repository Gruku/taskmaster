# Badge

A small Technical-voice status mark on a semantic tint (`color-*-subtle`, `radius-sm`).

**Consumer provides:** `children` (one or two words), `tone` (`success` · `warning` · `critical` · `info` · `accent` · `lime` · `pink`), `size` (`sm` · `lg`), `pill`, `pulse`.

- Semantic tones carry their shape (● ▲ ◆) — never color alone.
- `pulse` is a Pendulum rhythm (step-like, 2s), for live states only. Ambient motion must earn its place.
