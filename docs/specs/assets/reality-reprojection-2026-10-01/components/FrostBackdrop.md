# FrostBackdrop

An atmospheric container: signature, pink and lime radial orbs, blurred, under a grain layer, with its children lifted above.

**Consumer provides:** `children` (usually a card or frosted panel), `section` for the quieter section-level variant.

- Hue here is atmosphere, not meaning — use at most one per view, behind hero or showcase content.
- Gradients always carry grain (built in). Hidden in Survivalist.
