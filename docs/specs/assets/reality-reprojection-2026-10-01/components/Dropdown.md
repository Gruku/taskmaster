# Dropdown

A menu on `surface-overlay` with `shadow-elevated`; it opens from the top as a Flip Clock (a fall resolved into a snap).

**Consumer provides:** `label` (trigger), `items` (`{label, shortcut, active, disabled, children, onSelect}`, `'separator'`, `{header}`), `align="right"`, `defaultOpen`.

- Items shift sideways on hover; shortcuts in the Technical voice.
