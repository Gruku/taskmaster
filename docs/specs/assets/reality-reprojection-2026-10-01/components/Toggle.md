# Toggle

A physical switch: recessed track, machined thumb that snaps across on `ease-hourglass-settle`; active track fills with `signature-dim`.

**Consumer provides:** `label`, `checked`/`defaultChecked`, `onChange(next)`, `size="sm"`, `disabled`.

- Binary state is a Digital Clock moment — immediate commitment, cushioned by the settle.
- Keyboard: Space/Enter flips it; it is a `role="switch"`.
