# Declaration

The system's loudest voice — the name on the building, the word that fills the screen; it is the information rather than a label for it.

**Consumer provides:** `children` (short text), `level` (`hero` · `h1`–`h5`), optional `mode` (`"A"` default, `"B"` nature), `as` to change the tag.

- **Mode A** (default, League Spartan 800, `declaration-*` styles): uppercase, tight tracking (`tracking-tight`), `leading-tight`. Geometric, flat terminals, machined-well confidence.
- **Mode B** (`mode="B"`, Playfair Display 900, `declaration-alt-*`): sentence or title case, no tracking. The illuminated drop cap, the art-deco logotype, the gothic title card — for sensorial, historically-rooted projects.
- Modes A and B are **alternates, not companions**: pick one per project. They are endpoints of one dial; a future variable axis may morph between them.
- Follow every Declaration with `emitter-declaration` (96px) of clear space before the next group. It draws space toward itself.
- Must pass the **self-sufficiency test**: 1–3 letters on an empty surface still carry identity.
- Never mix with Narrator inside one element. No italic. Whole-pixel sizes only.
